# Setting Up the GitHub Student Developer Pack

In this tutorial you will verify your student status with GitHub Education, then activate the GitHub Student Developer Pack. It is a free bundle that upgrades your GitHub account and gives you access to dozens of professional developer tools while you are a student.

You do not need the Pack to use GitHub. A free account covers everything in [Hello_World_GitHub_VSCode.md](Hello_World_GitHub_VSCode.md). The Pack gives you more.

## What you will get

| Benefit | What it gives you |
| --- | --- |
| **GitHub Pro** | Free while you're a student. Raises your limits and adds tools for private repositories. |
| **GitHub Copilot Student** | An AI coding assistant with unlimited code completions, plus limited chat and agent use. |
| **GitHub Codespaces** | Pro-level cloud dev environments: 180 core hours and 20 GB of storage per month. |
| **GitHub Actions** | 3,000 automation minutes per month, up from 2,000. |
| **Private repository extras** | Pages, wikis, protected branches, required reviewers, and code owners. |
| **JetBrains** | A free subscription to professional IDEs such as IntelliJ IDEA and PyCharm. |
| **Free domains** | A free domain from Name.com, Namecheap (.me, with SSL), or .TECH, for a portfolio site. |
| **Cloud credits** | For example, Microsoft Azure ($100 credit, ages 18+), Heroku ($13 a month for 24 months), and MongoDB Atlas ($50). |
| **Learning platforms** | Free months of Educative, Frontend Masters, DataCamp, Boot.dev, and others. |
| **Productivity tools** | 1Password, Notion Education, Datadog, Sentry, GitKraken, and more. |

Offers change over time. See the full, current list at [education.github.com/pack](https://education.github.com/pack).

## Eligibility

You can apply if you:

- Are at least 13 years old
- Are enrolled in a degree- or diploma-granting program
- Can show proof of current enrollment

---

## Part 1: Prepare your account

Most rejections come from skipping this part. Finish every item before you apply.

- [ ] **Add and verify your school email.** Go to **Settings > Emails**, add your school email, and click the verification link GitHub sends.
- [ ] **Turn on two-factor authentication (2FA).** Go to **Settings > Password and authentication**.
- [ ] **Add your billing information.** Go to **Settings > Billing and plans** and enter your name and address. Your name must match your school documents exactly. Do not use a nickname.
- [ ] **Set your profile name** to the same full name.
- [ ] **Turn off any VPN.** GitHub checks your location against your school's location.
- [ ] **Have proof of enrollment ready** in case GitHub asks for it (see Part 3).

> **Tip:** After changing these settings, sign out of GitHub and sign back in before you apply.

## Part 2: Submit the application

1. Go to [github.com/settings/education/benefits](https://github.com/settings/education/benefits).
2. Click **Start an application**.
3. Select **Student**.
4. Choose your school email, or search for your school by name.
5. When your browser asks, **allow location access**. GitHub uses it to confirm you are near your school.
6. Describe how you plan to use GitHub, for example, "For coursework and class projects in my computer science program."
7. If GitHub asks for proof, follow Part 3. Otherwise, click **Submit**.

## Part 3: Upload proof of enrollment (if asked)

GitHub may ask you to photograph a document. Accepted documents, in order of preference:

1. Student ID card
2. Transcript
3. Enrollment or affiliation letter
4. Class schedule

**The document must show:**

- Your full first and last name, matching your GitHub billing name
- Your school's full name
- A date showing you are currently enrolled

**Photo tips:**

- Allow camera access and take a **live photo**. Do not reuse a photo from an earlier application.
- Use good light. Make sure all the text is sharp and readable.
- If your ID has no date, photograph it next to a dated document, such as your class schedule, in the same shot.

## Part 4: Wait for approval

- Applications are usually reviewed within a few days. Reviews take longer at the start of a semester.
- You will get an email with the decision. You can also check the status at [github.com/settings/education/benefits](https://github.com/settings/education/benefits).
- After approval, benefits usually appear within about 3 days.

## Part 5: Activate your benefits

**GitHub Pro** turns on automatically after approval. Check **Settings > Billing and plans**. Your plan should say **Pro**.

**Copilot Student** must be activated:

1. Go to [github.com/settings/education/benefits](https://github.com/settings/education/benefits).
2. Under **Free GitHub developer resources for students and teachers**, click **Learn more**.
3. Follow the prompts to activate Copilot Student.
4. In VS Code, sign out of GitHub and sign back in so Copilot picks up the new plan.

> **Important:** If Copilot shows only paid options, **do not buy a plan.** Wait a few days and try again. If it still doesn't work, contact GitHub Support.

**Partner offers** are claimed one by one:

1. Go to [education.github.com/pack](https://education.github.com/pack) and sign in.
2. Browse the offers and click **Get access** on the ones you want.
3. Each partner has its own sign-up steps. Many ask you to sign in with GitHub.

**Suggested first claims:** JetBrains (IDEs), a free domain for a portfolio, and one learning platform for a course you are taking.

## Keeping your benefits

- GitHub rechecks your eligibility regularly, monthly for Copilot Student. Keep your school email verified on your account.
- Benefits end when you are no longer a student. Partner offers have their own time limits, from one month to two years.
- Some partner offers start a countdown as soon as you claim them. Claim them when you are ready to use them.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Likely cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rejected: no date on proof | The document doesn't show current enrollment | Photograph your ID next to a dated class schedule or enrollment letter. |
| Rejected: name mismatch | Your billing name doesn't match your documents | Update your billing name to match your ID exactly, then reapply. |
| Rejected: location | VPN on, or applying far from campus | Turn off your VPN and allow location access. Online students should include proof of distance learning. |
| Rejected: school not found or wrong | School details are missing or incorrect | Search for the school by its full official name, or contact GitHub Education support. |
| Application page won't load or submit | Browser extensions or cache | Try another browser, clear the cache, or apply on your phone using mobile data. |
| Approved, but no benefits after 5 days | Benefits are still processing | Check **Settings > Billing and plans**, then ask in the GitHub Education community. |

Only reapply after you fix the reason for the rejection.

## Checklist

When you finish, you should have:

- [ ] A verified school email on your GitHub account
- [ ] An approved GitHub Education application
- [ ] **Pro** shown as your plan under **Settings > Billing and plans**
- [ ] Copilot Student active in VS Code
- [ ] At least one partner offer claimed
